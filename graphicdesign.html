What is Graphic Design?

Graphic Design is the art of creating visual content to communicate ideas, messages, and brand identity. Designers use typography, colors, images, and layouts to create logos, social media posts, advertisements, websites, and marketing materials.

Benefits of Learning Graphic Design

Graphic design helps you build creative skills, create professional branding materials, improve visual communication, and open career opportunities in marketing, advertising, freelancing, and digital media.
